Rain most of the afternoon, the kind that doesn't announce itself, just
arrives and stays. Read Ecclesiastes again — the part about there being a
season for everything — and thought about how rarely I let a season end on
its own instead of forcing the next one early. Made coffee twice. Only
drank half of the second cup. Put on Bill Evans, the Village Vanguard
recordings, and did nothing else for twenty minutes. Worth writing down
because it's rare enough to be worth writing down.
